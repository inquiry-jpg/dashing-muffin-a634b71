About Bright View
Real Estate

Under the leadership of Sandeep and Irshad, Bright View Real Estate is a growing Dubai-based real estate brokerage committed to delivering professional and client-focused property solutions. With industry experience since 2015 and over 11 years of combined expertise in Dubai's real estate market, the team has successfully rented and sold multiple residential properties across the city.

Founded with a vision to provide transparent, reliable, and result-driven real estate services, Bright View Real Estate specializes in property sales, leasing, and investment opportunities. The company is dedicated to helping tenants, buyers, landlords, and investors make confident real estate decisions through personalized service, market knowledge, and long-term relationships built on trust, integrity, and professionalism.
